03 MEET THE MUSICIAN

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm
Bryan.

I've been playing the cello for 14 years, and it has become one of the most meaningful ways I express myself. I studied orchestra at DSA, where I also took improvisation classes, and continued playing throughout elementary, middle, and high school.

I spent four years with the Utah Youth Symphony in Salt Lake City and have performed at weddings, church services, and other events. I love working with people to find music that feels right for their occasion.

Whether you have a specific song in mind or would like help choosing music, I'm happy to make the experience personal to you.
