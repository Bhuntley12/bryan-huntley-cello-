03 MEET THE MUSICIAN

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm
Bryan.

I've been playing the cello for 14 years, and it has become one of the most meaningful ways I express myself. I studied orchestra and improvisation at Denver School of the Arts (DSA).

I spent four years with the Utah Youth Symphony in Salt Lake City and have performed at weddings, church services, and other events. I love working with people to find music that feels right for their occasion.

Whether you have a specific song in mind or would like help choosing music, I'm happy to make the experience personal to you.

Can I request a specific song?

Yes—tell me the song and the moment you have in mind. I'm happy to discuss requests and work with you on music that feels personal to your day.

Where do you perform?

I'm based in Kaysville and available for events throughout Utah. Include your venue or city in your inquiry so we can discuss the location.

How much does live cello music cost?

Contact me for a personalized quote. Share the date, location, and approximate performance length, along with whether you'd like ceremony music, reception music, or both.

Can you help me choose music?

Absolutely. Whether you already have a favorite song or are starting from scratch, we can talk through your ideas and the atmosphere you'd like to create.

How do I check availability?

Use the inquiry form below, email bhuntley444@gmail.com, or call me. I'll discuss your date and event details with you.
